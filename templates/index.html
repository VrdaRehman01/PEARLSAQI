PUBLIC HEALTH CONTEXT

AQI health guide

Understand what each AQI range means for everyday activity.

0 — 50 Good

Air quality is satisfactory. Normal outdoor activities are appropriate.

51 — 100 Moderate

Acceptable conditions, with possible concern for unusually sensitive people.

101 — 150 Sensitive groups

Sensitive groups may experience health effects.

151 — 200 Unhealthy

Everyone may begin experiencing health effects.

201 — 300 Very unhealthy

Health alert conditions. Reduce prolonged outdoor exposure.

301+ Hazardous

Emergency conditions. Avoid outdoor exposure where possible.
