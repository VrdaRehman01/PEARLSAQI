Health Impact Guide

What each AQI range means and who it affects most.

0–50 · Good

Air quality is satisfactory. Enjoy normal outdoor activities.

51–100 · Moderate

Acceptable air quality. Unusually sensitive individuals should consider reducing prolonged outdoor exertion.

101–150 · Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups

Children, the elderly, and people with respiratory or heart conditions may experience health effects. General public is less affected.

151–200 · Unhealthy

Everyone may begin to experience health effects. Sensitive groups should avoid prolonged outdoor exertion.

201–300 · Very Unhealthy

Health alert: everyone may experience more serious health effects. Avoid outdoor activity where possible.

301+ · Hazardous

Emergency conditions. The entire population is likely to be affected. Stay indoors and keep activity levels low.

About this project

AQI Predictor forecasts Air Quality Index 1, 2, and 3 days ahead for Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Peshawar. All weather and pollutant data comes from Open-Meteo — no API key required anywhere in the pipeline. Historical training data spans 4 years of hourly readings per city. Four model types — Random Forest, Ridge Regression, XGBoost, and a TensorFlow neural network — are trained per forecast horizon, with the best-performing model (by RMSE) served automatically via the Hopsworks Feature Store and Model Registry. Predictions are explained per-request using SHAP, and compared against WHO Air Quality Guidelines.

Built with Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, TensorFlow, Hopsworks, Flask & Chart.js
